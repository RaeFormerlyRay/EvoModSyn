# Sample credits

Every sound in `samples/` is public domain (CC0), CC-BY with credit below, or synthesized by
`tools/make_samples.py`. There are no bells, vibraphones, glockenspiels or cowbells in the library.

## Sources

* **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Versilian Studios and contributors, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
* **Estuary samples**, collected for the Estuary live-coding platform, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/dktr0/estuary-samples (mirrored at github.com/felixroos/estuary-samples)
* **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm, CC-BY 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Recorded from a Yamaha C5. Here it is resampled to
  mono 22 kHz MP3 and trimmed. Index used to locate files: github.com/felixroos/dough-samples.
* **PatchMind synthesis**: kicks, 808s, organ, brass, plucks, vocal pads, vocal chops, risers,
  impacts and textures are generated by `tools/make_samples.py` and are released with this project.
  Breakbeats are original grooves played on the CC0 drums above, not copies of recorded breaks.
